Portfolio overview

Area of work

This project represents ongoing work in comparative enzymology, protein bioinformatics and computational investigation of pathogen biology. The portfolio records the scientific area and technical experience involved, while study-specific organisms, hypotheses, candidate molecules, datasets, numerical results and unpublished conclusions remain undisclosed.

Methods and skills

Capabilities represented

Comparative genomics and proteomics

Systematic comparison of genes, proteins and functional annotations across biological datasets.

Enzyme annotation

Functional classification, evidence organization and quality-aware interpretation of enzymatic activities.

Sequence bioinformatics

Homology searches, sequence filtering, multiple alignment and comparative protein analysis.

Structural bioinformatics

Use of structural evidence and protein representations to support comparative biological investigation.

Workflow automation

Reproducible pipelines, environment definition, provenance, validation and organized scientific outputs.

Scientific visualization

Publication-oriented workflows for figures, tables and structured computational research resources.
