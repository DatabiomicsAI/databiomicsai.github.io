Article abstract

Abstract

RSES-Onco Family-Aware is an eligibility- and coverage-aware multi-omics resource for prioritizing directional cancer vulnerabilities across non-homologous isofunctional enzymes, paralogs and alternative functional-backup families. The framework integrates tumor events, dependency, selectivity, expression compensation, functional relationships, functional microniche, validation and tractability, and family redundancy and backup architecture while distinguishing measured zero from unavailable, insufficient and non-applicable evidence. Cancer-specific regulatory, WGCNA, promoter-methylation, network, structural and pharmacological layers are linked to auditable pair- and family-level rankings. Source-backed controls, stratified bootstrap, cross-cancer validation, independent-method benchmarking and ablation are reported when estimable and otherwise remain explicitly unavailable. The interactive atlas at https://databiomics.com/New_articles/rses-onco-family-aware/ provides figures, source data, supplementary tables, validation status and reproducibility metadata for experimentally testable hypotheses.

Release safeguard

Current status

This landing page reserves the permanent resource URL and documents the intended scope. It does not publish provisional numerical findings. The analysis pipeline will replace this page only after the atlas has been generated from canonical RSES-Onco Family-Aware outputs and has passed its automated validation and completion gates.

  • Unavailable evidence remains unavailable and is not converted to zero.
  • Unknown or unmapped pairs are not treated as negative controls.
  • Ranked candidates are hypotheses for experimental validation, not clinical recommendations.
  • The website update is performed by an optional, reproducible pipeline parameter.
